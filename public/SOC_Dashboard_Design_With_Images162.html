S1
User with no prior removable drive or after-hours activity starts logging in after hours, using a removable drive, and uploading data to wikileaks.org. Leaves the organization shortly after.
  • Unrecognized thumb drive mounting events
  • Overnight activity shifts (10:00 PM – 6:00 AM)
  • Connect sequences matching exfiltration targets
S2
User searches job websites and solicits competitor employment. Before leaving, they heavily use a thumb drive to steal data, exceeding prior activity levels.
  • Hyper-burst activity patterns (rapid action gaps)
  • Spikes in activity during morning windows
  • Heavy thumb drive utilization rates
  • Corporate job search engine hits
S3
Disgruntled administrator downloads a keylogger, transfers it via thumb drive to supervisor's machine, uses keylogs to send mass email causing panic, then leaves.
  • Mass email distribution anomalies
  • Keylogger script download footprints
  • Logins to unauthorized or peer computers
  • Morning activity shifts suggesting sabotage
S4
User logs into another's machine, searches for files, and emails them to personal account, with increasing frequency over 3 months.
  • Unauthorized remote endpoint logins
  • Aggressive wildcard directory searches
  • Exfiltration routing to home email providers
  • Rapid sequence build-ups across quarters
S5
Multi-Vector Overlapping Activity Profile matching complex scenario combinations or data blending variations.
  • Concurrent deviations across multiple vectors
  • Correlated multi-vector baseline drift trends
  • Cross-domain indicator blending
S6

  • Isolated statistical volume spikes
  • No unauthorized logins, keyloggers, or leaks sites
  • Standard operational tasks occurring during safe windows
